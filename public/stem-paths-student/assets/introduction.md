# Introduction

This is a survey to evaluate probability and combinatoric math problems.  
The survey contains 3 math problems and a short questionnaire.

It will take approximately 45 minutes to finish this survey. 

For each question, we ask you to think-aloud as you solve each problem and then, you will be asked to evaluate your own approach and solution.
After, you will be asked to evaluate another approach and solution for each problem.

We suggest maximizing your browser window.
